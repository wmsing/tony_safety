In the Linux kernel, the following vulnerability has been resolved: crypto: algif\_aead - Revert to operating out-of-place This mostly reverts commit 72548b093ee3 except for the copying of the associated data. There is no benefit in operating in-place in algif\_aead since the source and destination come from different mappings. Get rid of all the complexity added for in-place operation and just copy the AD directly.

[View CVE Details](https://www.cve.org/CVERecord?id=CVE-2026-31431)
